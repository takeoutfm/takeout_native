# 016 — What the native apps include

**Date:** October 2026 (PLAN.md §7, as of commit 4bce83c) · **Status:** accepted

## Decision

- **Play next** and **custom radio stations** are in the first version (custom stations
  waiting on the server: there's no `POST /api/radio` route).
- **Picture-in-picture** for video on the phone.
- **A full native Wear OS app**, not a companion.
- The phone keeps the Flutter app's **floating play/pause button**.

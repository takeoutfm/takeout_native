# 014 — iPhone: iPhone conventions on Android's structure

**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** accepted

## Decision

Tabs as on the Android phone (Home · Artists · Recent · Radio · Search), drawn the iPhone way:
a Home of artwork rows, Now Playing as a swipe-down card tinted from the cover, swipe the cover
to skip, swipe actions on rows, haptics, AirPlay. The iPad uses the Mac's sidebar.

## Ruled out

- **The Mac's layout on the phone**, or **Android's look on iOS**.
